Process

How Our System Works

Simple, efficient, and completely automated

1

Waste Segregation at Source

Households, hotels, and markets segregate waste into three categories: Wet/Organic (biodegradable), Dry/Recyclable (plastic, paper, metal, glass), and Hazardous/Sanitary waste. We achieve ~70% household-level segregation through awareness campaigns.

2

Door-to-Door Collection

Our sanitation workers (Pourakarmikas) provide 97-100% coverage across all zones, collecting segregated waste daily using pushcarts and auto-tippers. Dry waste is collected separately for better processing.

3

Smart Transportation

GPS-tracked vehicles transport waste to transfer points or directly to processing facilities using optimized routes, ensuring efficient collection and reduced fuel consumption.

4

Wet Waste Processing

Organic waste is sent to large composting plants (Kesare, Vidyaranyapura, Rayanakere) with 550 tonnes/day capacity. Converted into high-quality compost for farmers or biogas, diverting ~60% of waste from landfills.

5

Dry Waste Recycling

Recyclables are sorted at Material Recovery Facilities (MRFs). Recyclables are sold to vendors, while multi-layer plastics are upcycled into Eco Pro Sheets or pavers through partnerships.

6

Zero Waste to Landfill

With 94% waste processing rate, minimal waste goes to sanitary landfills. Focus on achieving complete zero waste to landfill through continuous improvement and innovation.

7

Monitoring & Analytics

Real-time tracking, data analytics, and reporting ensure transparency. Citizens receive awareness training, and penalties enforce proper segregation for sustainable waste management.
